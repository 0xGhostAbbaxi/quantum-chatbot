About QuantumMind

AI + Quantum Chatbot

QuantumMind is a lightweight, cloud-powered chatbot that combines an AI language model with real quantum computing. Describe a problem in plain English — like grouping data into balanced sets, searching a small set of options, or getting a random pick — and QuantumMind figures out whether a quantum routine can help, runs it on IBM Quantum Cloud, and explains the result back to you in plain language.

How to use it: just type your problem into the chat box like you would with any normal chatbot. Use "New Chat" to start a fresh conversation — your past chats stay saved in the sidebar, with no session limit.
